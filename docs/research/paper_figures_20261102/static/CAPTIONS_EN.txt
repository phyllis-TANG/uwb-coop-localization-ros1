Main static figure

Per-robot effects of historical local range correction in five static normal bags. Panels (a,c) show position mean squared error (MSE) for independent localization (I) and full cooperation (J); panels (b,d) show D=MSE_J-MSE_I, where negative values indicate a relative cooperative benefit. Small markers and thin lines show individual bags; larger markers and bold lines show equal-weight bag means within each placement. G1 and G2 contain two and three bags, respectively, each with 15 reported common accepted evaluation samples. Raw and Historical denote uncorrected ranges and the fixed historical Car2 range correction. Whiskers show sensitivity to an assumed shared static-reference displacement of at most sqrt(2)*0.02 m, based on an operator estimate of 2 cm per coordinate; they are not statistical confidence intervals. The robot rows use different vertical scales. Results are a post-hoc comparison of saved static summaries; placement also changes propagation and initialization conditions and does not isolate pure geometry.

Calibration-effect figure

Change in relative cooperative effect after historical correction, C=D_historical-D_raw, for full cooperation J and fixed reduced-peer weight W025. Small markers and sensitivity whiskers represent the five individual static bags; large markers and connecting lines show the mean C within each placement. Positive C means that correction reduces the relative benefit of cooperation or increases its relative cost; it does not imply that the cooperative estimate's absolute MSE increases. Whiskers use the same shared-reference assumption as the main figure and account for the paired raw/historical mean-shift difference. They are sensitivity ranges, not confidence intervals. The samples within each bag are correlated and are not counted as independent experimental repetitions.
